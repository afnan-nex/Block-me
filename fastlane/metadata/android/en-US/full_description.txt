Block me is a 100% free, open-source, no-compromise focus timer for Android.

When you start a session, Block me locks your phone completely behind a persistent fullscreen overlay. You cannot exit until the timer expires.

FEATURES:
• Full-phone lockdown overlay during focus sessions
• Pressing Home, Back, or Recent Apps immediately locks the screen
• Session persists through screen off and device reboots
• Emergency phone calls still allowed via Phone button
• Wall-clock based timer — always ends at the exact calculated time
• Maximum 3-hour sessions
• Focus goal setting with quick-select recall
• Usage analytics — sessions, hours, streaks, temptation count
• Weekly bar chart and monthly heatmap
• Recurring scheduled focus sessions
• Haptic pulse every minute
• AMOLED pure-black dark mode

PRIVACY:
• Zero network calls
• All data stored locally
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no account
• We don't collect anything, period.
• GPL-3.0 open source — fully auditable

Unlike commercial focus apps that charge for strict mode, Block me is completely free with full strict mode included.
